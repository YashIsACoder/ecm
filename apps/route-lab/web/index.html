    How the strategy comparison works

    Before racing, a strategy team needs to know how much pace the battery can support when the weather changes. The lab draws possible conditions, runs all three policies on each draw, then measures how often each policy finishes or dips below 15% charge.

    Steady targets 82 km/h. Push targets 92 km/h. Reserve-first chooses 92, 85 or 75 km/h based on the energy it expects to spend on the current leg. Each strategy respects the leg’s cruise target cap. The intervals measure uncertainty from the simulated sample.
