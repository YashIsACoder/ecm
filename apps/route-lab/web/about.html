About the atlas

SolarRoute Studio is the browser atlas for the ECM C++ library. Route Atlas simulates Cruiser vehicles on shared weather draws. Live Race replays published checkpoint records from the 2025 event.

Sun and terrain

The native ephemeris starts on 24 August 2025 at 08:00 ACST. The map projects the sun vector into east, north and up at the selected car. Its compass direction changes the relief shadows, and elevation controls the transition through twilight.

Race timing

Simulated standings show distance driven at the replay time. The simulation includes control holds, overnight charging and late-stage timing under the modeled 2025 Cruiser constraints. Three distances are anchored to the published results. Intermediate route details and rival car inputs are estimates, and the comparison omits official design judging.
