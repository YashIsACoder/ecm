About the atlas

SolarRoute Studio is the browser atlas for the ECM C++ library. It follows five modeled Cruiser cars along a simplified Darwin–Adelaide route using a shared simulated weather draw.

Sun and terrain

The native ephemeris starts on 24 August 2025 at 08:00 ACST. The map projects the sun vector into east, north and up at the selected car. Its compass direction changes the relief shadows, and elevation controls the transition through twilight.

Race timing

Standings show distance driven at the replay time. The simulation includes control holds, overnight charging and late-stage timing under the modeled 2025 Cruiser constraints. The plotted route and car inputs are approximate, and the comparison omits official design judging.
